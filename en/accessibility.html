Accessible to everyone

Accessibility Statement

Accessibility is at the heart of Kishorit's worldview — in the village and here on the website.

Our commitment

Kishorit is a home for people with special needs, and making our services and information accessible to the whole public — including people with disabilities — is a core value for us. We work to keep this website usable, comfortable and clear for every person, in line with Israeli accessibility regulations (2013) and Israeli Standard 5568, based on the WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA.

Accessibility features on this website

  • Accessibility menu — a floating accessibility button on every page offers: larger text, contrast modes, grayscale, text spacing, increased line height, link highlighting, stopping animations, hiding images, a readable font and a large cursor. Your preferences are remembered between visits.
  • Keyboard navigation — the entire site can be operated with a keyboard only, with a clear focus indicator.
  • Semantic structure — ordered headings, alternative text for images, and ARIA labels for interactive components.
  • Color contrast — the site's colors were chosen to meet the required contrast ratios.
  • Reduced motion — the site honors the system preference for reduced animations (prefers-reduced-motion).
  • Two languages — the entire site is available in Hebrew and English.

Accessibility at the Kishorit village

The village is accessible for members, families and visitors — including accessible parking, paths and public buildings. To arrange an accessible visit, please contact us in advance at +972-4-9085111.

Found a problem?

If you found an inaccessible element or ran into any difficulty on this site, we would love to hear about it. Please include the page address, a description of the problem, and the browser and device you used — and we will handle it as quickly as possible.
