Donate to Kishorit

Kishorit constantly develops and innovates to improve its members' quality of life — and relies on the generosity of its supporters. Every contribution matters to us.

Kishorit is a registered public benefit company, recognized for tax-exempt donations in Israel, the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada.

A donation to Kishorit is not charity — it is an investment in a working, creative and independent community. You can designate your gift to a specific project (a working branch, housing, health or leisure) — write to us at kishorit1@kishorit.co.il.
