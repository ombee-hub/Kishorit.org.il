Our Industries

95% of Kishorit's members choose to work — in fourteen production and service branches that are part of village life and create real value.

Production branches

From the land, the animals and the studio

Winery & Visitor Center Boutique wines from our vineyard, and a visitor center open to the public.
Vineyard Growing the fine wine grapes behind Kishor wines.
Kennels One of Israel's leading dog breeding and training centers, including a boarding kennel.
Goat Dairy Goat milk and dairy products, tended daily by branch members.
Bakery Sourdough breads and fresh pastries for the dining hall and for sale.
Organic Vegetable Garden Fresh organic vegetables, from our soil to the plate.
Free-Range Coop Free-range eggs from hens roaming the village grounds.
Therapeutic Horse Farm Therapeutic riding and horse care — for the community and the wider public.
Kishorit TV A community television studio — filming, editing and broadcasting by the members.
Recycling & Plastics An industrial branch for plastics processing and recycling — good for the environment and for livelihood.

Service branches

The branches that keep the village running

Transportation

  • Driving members to work, treatments and outings across the Galilee.

Kitchen & Dining Hall

  • Three meals a day for the beating heart of community life.

Laundry

  • Laundry services for all the village homes and branches.

Maintenance

  • Caring for the homes, branches and public spaces.

About 10% of members work outside Kishorit in businesses across the region — by personal choice, with the community's support.
