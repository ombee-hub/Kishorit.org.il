Our worldview

Not an institution. A home.

Kishorit was founded on a simple belief: every adult deserves a home of their own, meaningful work and a community that sees them — for life.

A home for life

Not a temporary framework and not a "placement" — a permanent home in the Galilee, where members grow older with dignity, security and friendship.

Meaningful work

Every member works in a real branch — winery, kennels, bakery, community television — work that creates real value at the heart of village life.

An egalitarian community

Inspired by kibbutz life: shared decisions, a rich cultural and social life, and each person's own choice about how to live.

Our industries

A village that wakes up to work

Fourteen production and service branches operate at Kishorit — all run by community members, alongside the professional staff.

Winery & Visitor Center Boutique wines from our own vineyard, with a visitor experience open to the public.
Kennels One of Israel's leading dog breeding and training centers.
Goat Dairy Goat milk and dairy products, tended daily by branch members.
Bakery Sourdough breads and fresh pastries for the dining hall and for sale.
Organic Vegetable Garden Fresh organic vegetables, from our soil to the plate.
Therapeutic Horse Farm Therapeutic riding and horse care — for the community and the wider public.
Kishorit TV A community television studio — filming, editing and broadcasting by the members.
Free-Range Coop Free-range eggs from hens roaming the village grounds.

Alongside them — the vineyard, recycling and plastics, transportation, maintenance, the laundry, and the kitchen and dining hall. All our industries →
