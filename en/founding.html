Our story

The Story of Kishorit

From the dream of two people to a thriving Galilean community — a home for life, built on the lands of a kibbutz that had emptied of its residents.

Kishorit is the dream of Yael Shilo and Yehoshua (Shuki) Levinger, who worked to establish it until the dream became reality. They found a suitable site, secured the approvals for settlement, and built a close, ongoing partnership with the families' association.

Kishorit was founded in 1997 on the lands of Kibbutz Kishor, which had emptied of its residents. In the decades since, it has grown into a living, developing Galilean community.

"Kishorit is a home for life for one hundred and eighty-five people with a wide range of abilities, talents and passions" — adults with special needs in the developmental, emotional and psychological spheres.

Yael serves as chairperson and Shuki as CEO, and they remain a daily address for community members in every area of life — alongside a professional staff of some 150 people.

Milestones

1997

Kishorit is founded on the lands of Kibbutz Kishor in the Galilee, with a vision of a home for life for adults with special needs.

2000s

The working branches are established — the winery, kennels, dairy, bakery, vegetable garden and TV studio — and housing options across the village expand.

Today

A community of 185 members, 150 staff and 14 working branches — with an ongoing vision of an integrated village including Kibbutz Kishor and the planned village of Al-Fanara.

The spirit that drove the founders still guides Kishorit today: a belief in every person's right to a home, meaningful work, and a community that sees them.
