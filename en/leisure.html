Leisure Time

Leisure, classes and the arts

Kishorit devotes real attention to social and educational activity in leisure hours. The guiding principle: creating a wide, high-quality variety of activities that answer the personal wishes and needs of every member.

Leisure centers in the village

  • Members' club
  • Community pub
  • Synagogue
  • Gym and swimming pool

Classes

  • Sports classes
  • Arts — painting, music and theater
  • Enrichment and knowledge

Culture & community

  • Holidays and community events
  • Trips and outings across the Galilee
  • Culture evenings and performances

Healthy living

A significant part of the leisure program addresses common health issues — excess weight, diabetes and high blood pressure — through suitable nutrition and enjoyable, regular activity.

Physical activity

Structured training with a personal program for every participant, consistency tracking and close staff support — in the gym, the pool and across the village.

The environment

Kishorit strives to develop as a sustainable community: organic agriculture, recycling, and wastewater purification for reuse in irrigation — because our home is also the Galilee itself.
