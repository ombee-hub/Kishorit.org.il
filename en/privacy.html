Privacy Policy

What information is collected on the Kishorit website, how it is used, and what your rights are.

General

The Kishorit association ("Kishorit") respects the privacy of visitors to kishorit.org.il. This document describes what information is collected while using the site, how it is used, and how to contact us with any question on the matter.

What information is collected?

  • Information you provide — when sending an enquiry through the contact form or by email, the details you provide are kept: name, phone, email address and the content of your message. They are used solely to answer your enquiry.
  • Accessibility preferences — settings you choose in the accessibility menu (such as larger text or contrast) are stored only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • General usage data — the site's hosting provider may record anonymous technical information in server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed, for security and operations.

Third-party services

The site embeds content from external services, subject to their operators' privacy policies:

  • Vimeo — the video player on the homepage loads from Vimeo's servers only after you press play.
  • Waze — the navigation link on the contact page opens the Waze app.

Use and retention of information

Information provided in enquiries is used to respond and stay in touch with you only. Kishorit does not sell, rent or transfer personal information to third parties, except as required by law. We take reasonable, accepted measures to secure the information.

Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, you may review information kept about you and request its correction or deletion. For such requests, contact us at kishorit1@kishorit.co.il or +972-4-9085110.

Updates to this policy

Kishorit may update this policy from time to time. The binding version is the one published on this page.
